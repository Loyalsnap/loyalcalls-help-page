How to use LoyalCalls

An AI agent calls the clients you pick, pitches your offer, texts the purchase link, books them into class, and saves the transcript to their profile. It runs inside the portal you already use. Your call lists are built.

1

Open a list

Go to Lists › Dynamic lists. Any dynamic list works. We built two for you, and they refresh themselves every day:

  • LoyalCalls: never visited (60 to 120 days). People who created an account 2 to 4 months ago, never came in, never bought anything, and have nothing booked.
  • LoyalCalls: win-back, contract ended (30 to 365 days). Former members whose last membership ended 1 to 12 months ago, who have not bought anything since and have nothing booked.
2

Call with AI

The whole list

Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right of the list and choose Call with AI.

The option appears once the list has finished calculating. If it is missing, wait a moment and refresh.

Or specific people

Tick the clients you want. A Call with AI button appears at the bottom. Ticks carry across pages, up to 100 per run.

Then Start calling

Check who is being called and tap Start calling. That is the last thing you do; the agent takes it from here.

Good to know
  • 100 clients per run. On a bigger list, tick the ones you want. Anyone called in the last 7 days is skipped automatically, so come back to the same list for the next batch.
  • Calls go out 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM your local time, Monday to Saturday, a few at a time. A run started outside those hours waits for the window to open.
  • Skipped automatically: no valid mobile number, marked do-not-call, opted out of texts, already on an AI call, or called in the last 7 days.
  • The agent pitches your offer, texts the purchase link while the client is on the phone, and books them into a class. You can cancel a run at any time.
3

Check the stats

Tap AI calling in the left menu. Every call and every list run is here with its outcome.

Tap a run to see how the calls ended and each client's result: Booked Follow-up, Not interested, Voicemail, Not reached, and Not called (skipped). Tap Transcript on any row to read the call.

4

Read the transcript

Every call gets a short recap and the full transcript. Open it from the AI calling page, or from the client's profile: open the client, go to the Activity tab, and tap the AI phone call entry.

Booked clients show the class under Upcoming, and any text the agent sent shows in their conversation like any other message.

+

Calling one client

Open the client's profile, tap Call at the top of the Reach out panel, then the call button. Choose Have the AI agent call and tap Start AI call. The result and transcript land in their Activity tab the same way.

You can also call from your own browser or phone from the same dialog. The portal remembers your last choice.

Questions we get

What does the agent say?
LoyalCalls uses Loyalsnap Intelligence to shape each call around that client's history (last visit, membership, last package, recent texts) and your offer. There is no script to edit. Read a few transcripts after your first run to see how it adapts.
Which offer does it pitch?
The offer you gave us on the LoyalCalls form, at your price, with your purchase link. If you gave us more than one, it leads with the first and uses the others when they fit the person better. To change an offer, reply to your Loyalsnap contact.
Can it take payment?
No. It texts the purchase link during the call. Once the client has bought, the class is booked.
Who on my team can use it?
Anyone with the AI calling permission. Owners and everyone who can send SMS blasts already have it. For anyone else: Settings › Team, open the team member, switch on AI calling, save. Without it, Call with AI does not appear for that person.
Can I stop the AI from calling someone?
Yes. Mark the client do-not-call on their profile and they are skipped on every run.
Does it answer inbound calls?
Not yet. Outbound only for now. Inbound is planned for early December.
